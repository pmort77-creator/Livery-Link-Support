Livery-Link Privacy Policy Last Updated: 18 November 2025 Developer: Philip Mortimer Contact: pmort77@gmail.com Livery-Link (“the App”) is designed for livery yards, stables and equestrian clients to manage yard information and horse records. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is collected, used, stored, and protected. 1. Information We Collect 1.1 Information You Provide Horse Photos (Optional) Users can optionally upload or take a photo of their horse. These photos are stored in secure cloud storage and linked only to the specific yard account. Company Logos Livery businesses may upload a logo during onboarding. Account Information Name Email address Yard/business details (if you are a yard owner) We do not collect: Personal financial information Sensitive personal data Government IDs 2. How Your Data Is Stored Horse photos and company logos are stored in cloud storage in two secure locations: horse-photos bucket (horse images) company-logos bucket (business logos) These files are stored using industry-standard cloud storage, and each file has a unique URL. Although the buckets are publicly accessible by URL, they are not searchable and cannot be accessed without knowing the specific file’s URL. All other data (horse details, client information, yard details, etc.) is stored securely in our database. 3. How Your Data Is Used We use your data only to provide the features of the app, including: Displaying your horse’s image inside your yard’s account Identifying a yard through its logo Managing the livery yard’s internal information We do not: Sell your data Share your data with advertisers Track you across other apps 4. How You Can Delete Your Data You may delete your uploaded photos or account information at any time: Clients Delete horse photos from the horse’s profile Request full data deletion via email: pmort77@gmail.com Yard Owners Delete company logos in profile settings Request full account deletion at any time Upon receiving a deletion request, we will remove all associated data, including cloud-stored images, within 30 days. 5. Permissions the App Uses Camera Access (Optional) Used only to allow you to take a horse photo. Photos never leave your yard’s account unless you upload them. Photo Library Access (Optional) Used only when selecting an image from your gallery. The app will not access your camera or photo library without your permission. 6. Children’s Privacy This app is not intended for children under 13. No features target children, and no sensitive child data is collected. 7. Legal Basis Under GDPR For users in the UK/EU: We process data under the following legal bases: Performance of Contract — to provide the app services to you Consent — for optional uploading of images Legitimate Interest — to maintain secure functioning of the app You have the right to: Access your data Request deletion Correct your information Withdraw consent for optional photo uploads Requests can be made via: pmort77@gmail.com 8. Security We use industry-standard security practices, including: Secure cloud storage Encrypted connections (HTTPS) Access-limited database systems No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data to the highest practical standard. 9. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Updates will be posted here with a new “Last Updated” date. 10. Contact Us If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, contact: Philip Mortimer Email: pmort77@gmail.com
